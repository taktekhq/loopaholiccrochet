# Pricing — draft, needs Rana/Nizar sign-off

Every price in `catalog.json` is `price_is_draft: true`. They were set by comparing
each piece's size/complexity against real handmade listings, not invented:

- **Amigurumi plushies** (elephant, panda, bunny, teddy bear, etc.): Etsy median
  for handmade amigurumi plush is ~$13.75, with a wide spread ($2–$155) driven by
  size and complexity; mid-size (18–22 cm) animal plushies commonly sell $25–$50.
  Our plushies are priced $24–$28 for single animals, $30 for the boy doll and
  $42 for the two-doll mermaid set — inside that band.
- **Crochet flowers / single stems**: comparable single crochet flowers/stems sell
  $8–$24 (sunflower with wired stem ~$12, rose bouquets $8–$24). Ours are priced
  $12–$17 per stem/piece.
- **Baby teether rings** (wooden ring + crochet head): comparable listings range
  $9–$24 (median ~$11–$20). Ours are priced $16.

Sources checked 2026-10-08 (web search, Etsy/retailer listings for amigurumi
plush, crochet flower stems, and crochet teether rings — see workstream log for
the search queries).
