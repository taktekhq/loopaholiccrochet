Custom Orders

Want a color swap, a name, or a piece that doesn’t exist yet? We take commissions.

Most of our plushies and dolls can be made in different colors or sizes on request. We also make fully custom pieces — a name in crochet letters, a character, or a gift built around an idea you bring us.

Two examples below: crochet letters for a nursery, and a small flower keychain made as a party favor.

How it works

  1. Message us on Instagram or WhatsApp with what you have in mind (a photo or description helps).
  2. We confirm the design, price and a made-to-order time — custom pieces usually take longer than the shop catalog.
  3. You pay a deposit to start, we crochet it by hand, and send photos before it ships.
