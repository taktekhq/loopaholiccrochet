Custom orders

Want a color swap, a name, or a piece that doesn’t exist yet? We take commissions.

Start a custom order

Opens a direct message to @loopaholic.crochet.

Most of our plushies and dolls can be made in different colors or sizes on request. We also make fully custom pieces: a name in crochet letters, a character, or a gift built around an idea you bring us.

Two examples: a crochet letter for a nursery, and a small flower keychain made as a party favor.

How it works

  1. Tell us the idea

    Message us on Instagram with what you have in mind. A photo or a description helps.

  2. We agree the details

    We confirm the design, price and making time. Custom pieces usually take longer than the shop pieces.

  3. We make it by hand

    You pay a deposit to start, we crochet it, and send photos before it ships.
