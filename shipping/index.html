Shipping

Lebanon

Order on WhatsApp or Instagram. Pay by Whish transfer or cash on delivery. Delivery time depends on your area and how long the piece takes to make — we’ll confirm both when you order.

Gulf countries

We ship to the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman. Shipping cost is quoted on WhatsApp or Instagram once we know your city and the pieces you want — we haven’t fixed flat rates yet.

Everywhere else

We ship internationally. Shipping cost is quoted on WhatsApp or Instagram until our checkout can calculate it automatically.

Good to know

Every piece is made to order, so shipping starts after the made-to-order time on the product page, not the day you order. Customs fees outside Lebanon are the buyer’s responsibility.
