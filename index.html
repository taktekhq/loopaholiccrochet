How ordering works

There’s no cart and no checkout. You order in a conversation with the person who makes it.

  1. Pick a piece

    Browse the shop, or bring an idea for a custom order.

  2. Message us

    On Instagram. We confirm the price, colors and making time with you there.

  3. We make it by hand

    Once the price and colors are agreed, we crochet your piece and arrange delivery.

Made by hand in Lebanon

Every plushie, doll and flower is crocheted by hand, one stitch at a time, after you order it. Small differences in a stitch or a shade of yarn are part of what makes each one yours.
