About the craft

Loopaholic makes amigurumi — crocheted, stuffed figures — and crochet flowers entirely by hand, one stitch at a time. Nothing is machine-made or mass-produced: every plushie, doll and flower on this site is handmade in Lebanon and made to order.

Because each piece is made by hand after you order it, small variations — a slightly different stitch, a shade of yarn — are part of what makes it one of a kind, not a flaw.
