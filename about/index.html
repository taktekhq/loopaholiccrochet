About the craft

Every piece is crocheted by hand in Lebanon, one stitch at a time.

Loopaholic makes amigurumi — crocheted, stuffed figures — and crochet flowers entirely by hand, one stitch at a time. Nothing is machine-made or mass-produced: every plushie, doll and flower on this site is handmade in Lebanon and made to order.

Because each piece is made by hand after you order it, small variations, like a slightly different stitch or a shade of yarn, are part of what makes it one of a kind, not a flaw.

Follow the latest pieces and works in progress on Instagram: @loopaholic.crochet

How ordering works

  1. Pick a piece

    Browse the shop, or bring an idea for a custom order.

  2. Message us

    On Instagram. We confirm the price, colors and making time with you there.

  3. We make it by hand

    Once the price and colors are agreed, we crochet your piece and arrange delivery.
