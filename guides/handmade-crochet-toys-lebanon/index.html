Handmade crochet toys in Lebanon

Loopaholic crochets amigurumi plushies, dolls and baby pieces by hand in Lebanon, and makes each one after you order it. To order, send an Instagram direct message to @loopaholic.crochet. There are no prices on the site: the price, colors and making time are confirmed with you in the chat.

Amigurumi plushies, crocheted by hand

Amigurumi are crocheted, stuffed figures. The shop has a whole menagerie of them: an elephant, a panda with a small flower clip, a pink bunny, an octopus, a lion, a koala, a giraffe, a puppy, a monkey, a cow, a sea turtle, a shark on a stand and a classic teddy bear. Nothing is machine-made or mass-produced; each one is crocheted by hand, one stitch at a time, once you order it.

  • Pink Bunny Amigurumi Plush
  • Octopus Amigurumi Plush

Crochet dolls

The dolls include the Mermaid Twins, two mermaid dolls sold together, one with a purple tail and one with a pink tail; an Amigurumi Boy Doll; a Ballerina Doll in a lilac tutu; and a Ballerina Mouse in a pink tutu. Like every piece in the shop, each doll is made to order in Lebanon.

Baby pieces

The baby section has three crocheted teether rings: a Duck Teether Ring, a Lamb Teether Ring and an Elephant Teether Ring. Our baby pieces haven’t been through any testing, so we make no claim about which ages they suit. Message us before ordering for an infant, and always supervise young children with any small handmade item.

How ordering works

There’s no cart and no checkout. You pick a piece and message @loopaholic.crochet on Instagram. In the chat we confirm the price, colors and making time with you. Once the price and colors are agreed, we crochet your piece by hand and arrange delivery. In Lebanon you pay by Whish transfer or cash on delivery.

A different color, or a piece that doesn’t exist yet

Most of our plushies and dolls can be made in different colors on request. We also take custom orders: a name in crochet letters, a character, or a gift built around an idea you bring us. Tell us the idea on Instagram, we agree the design, price and making time, you pay a deposit to start, and we send photos before it ships.

FAQ

How much does a handmade crochet toy cost?

We don’t list prices on the site. Message us on Instagram with the piece you want and we’ll confirm a price before you order.

Do you deliver in Lebanon?

Yes. Pay by Whish transfer or cash on delivery. Delivery time depends on your area and how long the piece takes to make; we confirm both when you order.

How long does an order take to make?

We confirm the making time when you message us. Custom orders usually take a bit longer.

Are the pieces suitable for babies and small children?

Our baby pieces haven’t been through any testing, so we make no claim about which ages they suit. Message us before ordering for an infant, and always supervise young children with any small handmade item.
