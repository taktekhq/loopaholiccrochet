Crochet amigurumi gift ideas, by occasion

A crochet amigurumi gift is a crocheted, stuffed figure made by hand, so no two are quite alike. Loopaholic crochets each piece to order in Lebanon: teether rings and plushies for a new arrival, dolls for birthdays, crochet flowers, and fun pieces like a sushi duo. Order by Instagram message to @loopaholic.crochet.

For a new baby

For a new arrival, the baby section has a Duck Teether Ring, a Lamb Teether Ring and an Elephant Teether Ring, and the plushies include a classic teddy bear and a pink bunny with long floppy ears. Our baby pieces haven’t been through any testing, so we make no claim about which ages they suit: message us before ordering for an infant.

  • Duck Teether Ring
  • Elephant Teether Ring
  • Lamb Teether Ring
  • Classic Teddy Bear Plush

For a birthday

Dolls and plushies make a birthday gift that feels personal: the Mermaid Twins (two dolls sold together), the Ballerina Doll, the Ballerina Mouse, a lilac koala with its arms out for a hug, or a giraffe with brown spots. Want the birthday child’s name? A name in crochet letters is one of the custom orders we take.

Make it personal, then order

Ask for a different color, a name, or a piece that doesn’t exist yet. Past custom pieces include a crochet letter for a nursery and a small flower keychain made as a party favor. To order any gift, message @loopaholic.crochet on Instagram: we confirm the price, colors and making time in the chat, then crochet it by hand.

FAQ

How do I order a crochet gift?

There’s no cart or checkout. Message @loopaholic.crochet on Instagram with the piece you want. We confirm the price, colors and making time with you there, then crochet it by hand and arrange delivery.

Can I choose the colors?

Usually yes. Message us with the colors you’d like and we’ll confirm if it works for that piece.

Can you send a gift outside Lebanon?

Yes. We ship to the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman, and internationally. Shipping cost is quoted on Instagram once we know your city and the pieces you want. Customs fees outside Lebanon are the buyer’s responsibility.

How much does a crochet gift cost?

We don’t list prices on the site. Message us on Instagram with the piece you want and we’ll confirm a price before you order.
